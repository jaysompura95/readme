## Part A — 25 Companies

**City:** Hyderabad  
**Segments:** Specialty Biotech (Probiotics/Fermentation/Recombinant Proteins) + Specialty Diagnostics & Life Science Tools  

**Why Hyderabad:**
Genome Valley alone has 200+ life science companies. Highest density of specialty biotech + diagnostics companies in India with genuine operational presence (R&D, manufacturing, founders) — not just registered offices.

**ICP Criteria Applied:**
1. Actually manufactures specialty products (not trading/distribution)
2. Specialty segment — NOT generic/bulk/commodity pharma
3. Size fit: ₹5Cr – ₹500Cr revenue; 10–500 employees
4. Not PE-owned, not MNC subsidiary
5. Founder/promoter-led company
6. Operational presence in Hyderabad (not just registered office)

**Confidence Tiers in CSV:**
- 🟢 **HIGH** — Verified from 2+ primary sources
- 🟡 **MEDIUM** — Verified from 1 source; 1 more verification recommended
- 🔴 **LOW** — Found in directory; MUST verify website + products before pitching

**Expected yield rate:** ~30% (consistent with DT's stated benchmark)  
**Companies researched to find 25:** ~80
